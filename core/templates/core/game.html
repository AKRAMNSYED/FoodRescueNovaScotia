DO YOU KNOW ABOUT HUNGER INSECURITY?

HERE ARE SOME FACTS:

  1. 26.2% of people in Nova Scotia have food insecurity!
  2. 34.8% of children in the province live in food-insecure households, reflecting high rates of youth and family economic vulnerability.
  3. The main source of food insecurity in Nova Scotia is inadequate income, driven by low wages, high living costs, and social assistance rates that fall below the poverty line!
  4. Single-parent families face the highest burden, with over 52% to 57% experiencing food insecurity.
  5. Some people are making “billions of dollars off of food insecurity” and overpriced food!
