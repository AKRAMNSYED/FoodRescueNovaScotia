Nova Scotia Hunger Insecurity

Food insecurity remains a serious issue across Nova Scotia, affecting households, families, students, and older adults. Access to nutritious food is not only a basic need, but a foundation for health, stability, and opportunity.

Hunger insecurity impacts families and communities across Nova Scotia every day.

Food Access

Many families are forced to make difficult choices between food and essentials.

Community Support

Local programs, food banks, and advocates work to reduce barriers and build resilience.

Action Needed

Continued awareness, outreach, and support can help communities move toward food security.

Rates of Hunger Insecurity in Nova Scotia

1 in 7 households experience food insecurity
18% of families report food access challenges
25% of children may face food insecurity risk
